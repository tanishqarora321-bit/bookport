K

Interaction rules

Hover
Rows tint to surface-2; editable cells reveal the pencil. Nothing moves or resizes.
Focus
2 px focus ring, 2 px offset, on every control. Tab order follows reading order.
Pressed
Buttons darken one step (accent → accentHover). No scale effects.
Disabled
45% opacity, not-allowed cursor, and a tooltip saying why (e.g. "No seats left").
Keyboard
Enter saves an inline edit, Esc cancels, arrows move in menus, Esc closes dialogs and returns focus.
Confirm
Irreversible actions use a dialog naming the record and the consequence; the danger button repeats the verb.
Async
Show "…ing" text and lock the button; success only after the server confirms; keep input on failure.
Motion
120–180 ms fades only; respect prefers-reduced-motion.
